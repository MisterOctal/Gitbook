# Training Impact on Teams

**Date:** 05.04.2026

**Room Category:** Walkthrough

**Core Concept:** Understanding how cyber security training increases organizational capacity, improves junior onboarding, and calculating the financial Return on Investment (ROI) for training programs.

Cyber security is a skill mastered through practice. Platforms like TryHackMe provide safe, isolated environments (labs) to practice techniques that would otherwise be too risky to perform on production systems. Training is a proactive measure that reduces the impact and likelihood of live security incidents.

***

## Task 1: Understanding the Impact of Cyber Security Training

Training is the primary way to build and maintain expert skills in a rapidly evolving field. For an organization, the "real power" of training is that it increases the team's capacity without needing to hire additional staff.

<figure><img src="https://tryhackme-images.s3.amazonaws.com/user-uploads/5f04259cf9bf5b57aed2c476/room-content/5f04259cf9bf5b57aed2c476-1729072001059.svg" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

**Key Benefits:**

* **Preparedness:** Teams learn tools and techniques in a lab rather than during a live attack.
* **Onboarding:** Training makes it feasible to hire junior staff and ramp them up quickly, reducing the burden on senior mentors.
* **Common Baseline:** Centralized training provides a clear metric for assessing skills (replacing vague terms like "junior" or "senior").
* **Teamwork:** CTF (Capture the Flag) challenges foster friendships and collaboration.

Question: What is the most efficient way to ramp up the skills of a junior hire in cyber security?

> **Answer:** Training

## Task 3: Write a Cyber Security Training Investment Proposal

To secure a budget, security professionals often need to demonstrate the financial value of training to leadership.

<figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/image (22).png" alt="" width="563"><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

#### Calculating ROI (Return on Investment)

The financial impact is calculated by looking at the productivity gains versus the cost of the training.

**Scenario Example:**

* **Team:** 10 employees.
* **Salary:** $80,000/year per employee.
* **Productivity Gain:** 4% via training.
* **Total Gain:** $32,000 ($800k total salary × 0.04).
* **Cost:** $500 per employee ($5,000 total).
* **ROI:** 640% ($32,000 / $5,000).

Question: Situation: A cyber security team has 20 employees. Cost: Each employee costs $50,000 annually. Assumption: Training increases productivity by 4%. What would be the savings due to the increased productivity?

> **Answer:** 40000

Question: Assuming that training costs $500 per employee, what is the Return on Investment?

> **Answer:** 400%
