Hello!

I'm Wai Yan Lin Khant, though you might know me online as MisterOctal (or just Octal). I am a Network Engineering HND student based in Myanmar, actively working towards a career as a cybersecurity professional. I'm a firm believer in hands-on practice, which is why I spend my time building labs, breaking things, and figuring out how they work.

My fascination with cybersecurity started when I was a kid. Technology is so prevalent in our daily lives, yet we often take its security for granted. I have always been intrigued by the offensive side - how systems are compromised but I am equally driven by the defensive side: understanding how we can learn from those attacks to prevent them from happening again.
